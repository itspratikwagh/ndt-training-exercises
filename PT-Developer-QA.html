Process Control · Materials QA

Will the developer still do its job?

The developer is the last material a part sees — it draws penetrant back out of defects. Two forms, two simple checks:

  • Dry powder: it must be fluffy and free-flowing (moisture cakes it), and clean under UV-A — every glowing speck is dragged-in penetrant, and the limit is 10 specks inside the marked circle.
  • Aqueous developer: concentration is checked with a hydrometer — read the specific gravity at the surface line; limits 1.040–1.080, target ≈1.060.
  • There are four scenarios to work through — keep hitting “Next week” until you've seen all four.
