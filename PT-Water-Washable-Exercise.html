Practical Exercise · Liquid Penetrant Testing · Method A

Inspect a welded plate — water washable, fluorescent

Scope: perform PT on the butt weld below using Type 1 fluorescent, water-washable penetrant, and evaluate under UV-A light.

Area of inspection: the entire weld length plus 1 inch on either side to cover the HAZ. It is marked on the plate.

  • The excess penetrant comes off with a water spray, not a rag — and the wash station must be set per procedure before you spray.
  • Washing happens under UV-A so you can see when the fluorescent background is gone. Over-washing pulls penetrant back out of flaws — stop when the background is clean.
  • Developer is aqueous: dip the part while it’s still wet, then dry it in warm air.
Dwell timers are accelerated: 10 min runs in ~45 s. In the real shop you wait the full time.
