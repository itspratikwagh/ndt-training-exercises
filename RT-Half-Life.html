Activity

Activity = disintegrations per second

Activity = λ × N. For one isotope, λ is fixed, so activity depends only on N, the number of radioactive atoms.

Divide by 2, again and again

After 1 HLA0 ÷ 2
After 2 HLA0 ÷ (2 × 2)
After 3 HLA0 ÷ (2 × 2 × 2)

A0 = Original Activity

New Activity = Original Activity (A0) ÷ 2nn = No. of days ÷ Half-life

Three steps every time: find n, work out 2n, then divide.
