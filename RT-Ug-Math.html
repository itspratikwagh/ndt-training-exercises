Where the formula comes from

Two triangles, same shape

Top triangle: width F, height SOD.
Bottom triangle: width Ug, height OFD.
They meet at the object's edge, so their angles match.
Ug = F × OFD ÷ SODF = source (focal spot) size · OFD = object-to-film distance · SOD = source-to-object distance

Turn it around

The code sets Ug. You set the distance.

F and OFD are usually fixed: F comes with the source, and OFD comes from the part.

Ug = F × OFD ÷ SOD
Min SOD = F × OFD ÷ Ug
Min SFD = SOD + OFD
