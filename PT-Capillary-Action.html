Penetrant Testing · Theory

Why does penetrant need dwell time?

Nothing pushes penetrant into a crack. The liquid wets the crack walls and pulls itself in — that's capillary action, and it takes time.

  • Spray the penetrant across the part, then watch it wick into the two cracks.
  • The shallow crack fills quickly. The deep, tight one keeps drawing penetrant for minutes.
  • You decide when to wipe off the excess. The procedure calls for a 10-minute dwell — see what happens if you cut it short.
  • Then spray the developer and watch what's trapped in each crack bleed back out.
