§ 20.1003 · ALARA
As Low As Reasonably Achievable
Make every reasonable effort to keep exposures well below the dose limits, not just under them.
"Reasonable" takes into account:
The state of technology
Economics
The benefit to public health
§ 20.1003 · Body regions
Whole body vs. extremities

Whole body: your core

Head, trunk (including the male gonads), arms above the elbow, legs above the knee

Extremities: elbows & knees down

Elbow, arm below the elbow, hand, knee, leg below the knee, foot

The elbow and knee themselves count as extremities
