Material Verification · Occasional Check

Is the penetrant itself still good?

The quench-cracked aluminum comparator is one block, cracked by heating and water-quenching, divided into two matched halves — A and B. Because both halves carry the same crack network, processing two penetrants side by side under identical conditions isolates one variable: the penetrant.

  • Apply the in-use penetrant to side A and fresh reference penetrant to side B.
  • Same dwell, same wash, same developer — then compare the two halves under UV-A.
  • This is an occasional check, done when penetrant condition is in question — not the daily TAM panel check.
