MT · One Coil, Two Jobs

Make a magnet — then unmake it

The same bench coil magnetizes and demagnetizes. The difference is the current, and what the part does:

  1. SHOT: part inside the coil, wet it with bath, two ½-second shots — one steady burst. The particles pile up at the ends: it's a magnet now.
  2. DEMAG: flip the selector — continuous AC. Coil on, pull the part through and past the line, coil off.
  3. Check with the field indicator — under 3 G passes.
