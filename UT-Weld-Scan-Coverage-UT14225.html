Weld Scan: Full Coverage with a 45° Wedge

← Exercise Hub

FlawTech plate UT-14225, 5 MHz probe on a 45° wedge. Step by step: read the plate, find the skip, then place the probe to cover the root, the middle, the toes and the HAZ, from both sides.

Exit point to weld centerline---
Drag the probe along the plate. Distances are measured from the exit point to the weld centerline.
Steps
    ✓ Done. Scan 0.40″ to 1.15″ from the weld centerline, from both sides.

    Key points

    • 45° wedge: the sound moves forward as much as it goes down. Half skip = thickness (0.375″), one skip = 2 × thickness (0.750″).
    • Leg 1 reaches the root. Leg 2 covers the middle, the top and the toes.
    • Scan zone on this plate: exit point 0.40″ to 1.15″ from the weld centerline. You can't get closer: the wedge hits the crown.
    • Include the HAZ (heat-affected zone), the base metal right next to the weld.
    • One side covers about 90%. Scan from both sides to cover all of it.
