Take the magnetism back out

A part leaves MT still magnetized — bad for welding, machining and bearings. The fix takes one slow move:

  1. Check the part with the field indicator.
  2. Hold the yoke's trigger, touch the part, pull away slowly — let go only past the line.
  3. Check again. Under 3 G passes.
