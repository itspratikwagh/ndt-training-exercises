Practical Exercise · Liquid Penetrant Testing · Method A

Run a part through the water washable line

Scope: process the welded plate through a Method D post-emulsifiable line: a water pre-rinse knocks down the bulk, then a hydrophilic (water-based) remover with gentle agitation takes the rest. Evaluate under UV-A.

How it works: the part hangs from an overhead carrier. Drag it along the rail to move between stations, and drag it down to lower it into a tank — you'll see it go under and come back out.

  • Stations, in order: water pre-wash · penetrant tank · drain rack (dwell) · pre-rinse booth · hydrophilic remover tank · fresh-water rinse under UV-A · forced-air dryer · dry-powder dust chamber · development rack · inspection booth.
  • Plain water can only take 60–80% of the surface penetrant — the remover does the rest, at the right concentration and with slight air bubbling only.
  • After the remover, a fresh-water rinse must follow immediately to stop its action.
  • The stations are set up at shift start, so the stop-rinse can follow the remover without delay.
  • Dwell times matter both ways: once a timer finishes, a red clock starts — don't let the part sit.
  • The part must be completely dry before the dust chamber — set the oven per procedure, run the cycle, then verify with the thermal gun.
Dwell timers are accelerated: 10 min runs in ~45 s. In the real shop you wait the full time.
